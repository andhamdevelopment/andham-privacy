Privacy Policy

Effective Date

This Privacy Policy is effective as of July 2026.

1. Introduction

Andham ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Andham mobile and web application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information: • Mobile number (required for account creation) • Full name and profile picture • Address and city (for service area matching) • Device token (for push notifications) • Portfolio photos and video clips (for artist profiles) • GPS location (for nearby artist discovery — only when permission is granted) • Booking and payment history • Chat messages (stored for 6 months)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: • Create and manage your account • Connect clients with nearby artists • Process bookings and payments • Send push notifications for bookings, chats, and payments • Improve and personalise the App experience • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with: • Artists/clients as necessary to fulfil bookings • Razorpay for payment processing • Firebase (Google) for push notifications • Azure (Microsoft) for secure cloud storage • Law enforcement when required by law

5. Location Data

We request location permission to show nearby artists. Location is only accessed when you actively use the nearby search feature. We do not track your location in the background.

6. Media Storage

Photos and video clips you upload are stored securely on Microsoft Azure Blob Storage. Profile pictures and portfolio media are publicly accessible via URL to enable sharing and discovery on the platform.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Chat messages are automatically deleted after 6 months. You may request deletion of your account by contacting support.

8. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including: • JWT-based authentication with token rotation • PIN stored as SHA-256 hash (never plain text) • HTTPS encryption for all API communications • Azure-managed secure storage

9. Your Rights

You have the right to: • Access the personal data we hold about you • Correct inaccurate data • Request deletion of your account and data • Withdraw consent for location access at any time via device settings

10. Children's Privacy

Andham is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Third-Party Links

The App may contain links to third-party services (e.g. Instagram, YouTube). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those services.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the App. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise your rights, please contact: support@andham.app
